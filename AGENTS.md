- Cloud Worker packs auratok-cloud-worker/ to public/auratok-cloud-worker.zip; polls /api/public/cloud-worker with CLOUD_WORKER_SECRET. LIVE needs open matching room; reports match owner/username/revision. Saves clear timestamps; guarded callbacks/serialized status reject stale reports. Startup build ID verifies version; level logs exclude identities/payloads.
- Normalize raw gift/like identities in live-events.js; Tower receives source delta counts and confirmed room generations through existing ingress, never aggregate goal batches, so room totals and old-room deliveries cannot inflate gameplay.
- Gift Scout (scout.js/scout-gifts.js): polls /api/public/gift-scout with the worker secret, connects briefly to one queued public room at a time and reports mapRoomGifts output; offline rooms stay 'offline' until an admin requeues.
- Battle task diagnostics are passive, explicitly deadline-enabled and capped at 30 minutes/120 samples per worker session; allowlisted task numbers/translation keys only, no IDs/text/URLs/raw bytes.
- Speed Challenge (speed-challenge.js) activates only at TikTok's official targetStartTimestamp from battleTask start messages, deduped per battle task and reported to /api/public/booster-task with TikTok's official rewardMultiple (x2/x3) from the same start message; never guessed from scores, timers or task configuration, and close() cancels pending reports when a session stops.
- Discover jobs every 5 seconds but heartbeat unchanged sessions every 20 seconds; one serialized status sender publishes transitions immediately and acknowledges exact reports only, preserving newer transitions and retries.
- connect-budget.js is the single guard for the signing service's connection quota: a rate-limit answer pauses all new creator and Gift Scout connects until the service's reset (capped ~1 hour), attempts are spread to 2 per 5 seconds, and a signing-free LIVE pre-check skips full connects for offline creators (full check at least every 5 minutes); open sockets are never touched.
